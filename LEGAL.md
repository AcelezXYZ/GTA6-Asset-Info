# Legal notes

This page is not legal advice. Laws on reverse engineering, interoperability
and modding differ between countries. Check what applies where you live.

## What SixNG is for

SixNG is a tool for **interoperability and modding research**: reading the
data formats of a game you own so that you can study them and use your own
copy's content in mods for your own use.

## Your own legally obtained copy only

- SixNG works only on game files from **your own, legally obtained copy**
  of the game, read from your own install.
- It does not download, unlock or decrypt anything. It does not bypass
  copy protection, and it cannot read the encrypted parts of the archives.
- You are responsible for how you obtain your game files and for what you
  do with the output.

## What SixNG contains, and does not

SixNG and this repository contain **none** of the following:

- game files, archives or parts of them;
- extracted assets (models, textures, audio, map data, text);
- encryption keys or other secrets;
- proprietary or NDA-covered SDK material, documentation, tools or code.

All format knowledge in these docs was worked out from the data itself and
from public information.

## Sharing output

Assets extracted from the game remain the property of their owners.
**Do not redistribute extracted assets** (models, textures, maps, audio)
unless you have the right to do so. The format notes, the tool's source
code and your own original work are what this project shares.

If you publish a mod, follow the rules of the platform you publish on and
the game publisher's policies on modding.
