# Legal notes

This page is not legal advice. Laws on reverse engineering, interoperability
and modding differ between countries. Check what applies where you live.

## What this repository is for

These are **interoperability and modding research notes**: descriptions of
the data formats GTA VI uses, so that people can understand and work with
the files of copies they own.

## What it contains, and does not

This repository is documentation, plus the original-code `scripthookvi/`
framework (no game code, no patterns for the game, no anti-cheat bypass).
It contains **none** of the following:

- game files, archives or parts of them;
- extracted assets (models, textures, audio, map data, text);
- encryption keys or other secrets;
- proprietary or NDA-covered SDK material, documentation, tools or code.

The notes were worked out from the data itself and from public
information. Short identifiers that appear in them (structure offsets,
hash values, flag names, sample asset names) are there to describe the
formats, not to reproduce game content.

## Using these notes

- Work only with files from **your own, legally obtained copy** of the
  game.
- Nothing here explains how to obtain game files, decrypt protected data or
  bypass copy protection, and contributions that do will not be accepted.
- You are responsible for how you obtain your game files and for what you
  do with anything you make from them.

## Sharing what you make

Game assets remain the property of their owners. **Do not redistribute
extracted assets** (models, textures, maps, audio) unless you have the
right to do so. If you publish a mod, follow the rules of the platform you
publish on and the publisher's policies on modding.
